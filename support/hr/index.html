Holo support

Holo is an app for trading cards: scan your cards, see what they are worth and keep track of your collection. Your collection stays on your iPhone and in your private iCloud.

Send feedback

Have an idea, found a bug or got a question? Open Holo › Settings › Send feedback, pick a type and write a few lines. Add your email if you would like a reply.

Delete your public data

Open Holo › Settings › Delete my public data. This removes your friend profile, listings, wishes, photos and sent feedback from the public database and pauses sharing. Your own collection is not touched. To delete everything, delete the app and Holo's data in iCloud.

Privacy

Holo has no ads and no tracking, and we never sell data.
