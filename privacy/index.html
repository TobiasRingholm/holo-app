Privacy in Holo

Updated 2026-11-13

In short

Holo has no ads and no tracking, and we never sell data. You don't create an account with us – Holo uses your Apple Account and your own iCloud.

Your collection

Cards, folders, photos, receipts, sales, decks and wishes are stored on your iPhone and in your private iCloud at Apple, so they move with you to a new phone. The developer can't see your private iCloud.

Camera and photos

Cards are recognised on your phone. For look-ups, only the card's name, number and set are sent to open card databases (such as TCGdex and Scryfall) – never your picture. Photos you save stay in your collection.

Prices and currency

Holo fetches public price lists and exchange rates (such as Cardmarket, eBay through Holo's own server, and Frankfurter). Only the card's name or number and your country are sent – no personal information.

Friends

You get a friend code. Holo puts your display name and an overview of your collection (cards and value) in Holo's public iCloud database, so people with your friend code can see it. Only share the code with people you know.

The open market

The market is off until you turn it on, and it is for adults only (age is checked with Apple's age check where possible). When it is on, other Holo users can see your listings, photos of the card, wishes, an email for contact and an approximate location (rounded to about 5 km, country and city). Contact happens by email only – there is no chat in the app. You can remove listings and turn the market off at any time.

Feedback

If you write to us in the app, your message, a reply email (if you enter one) and the app version, language, country, currency, and iPhone and iOS version are sent via iCloud. This is only used to reply to you and fix bugs.

Holo Plus

Purchases are handled by Apple. We receive no payment details. Your purchase is restored through your Apple Account, also on a new phone and with Family Sharing.

Deleting

Delete cards and folders in the app. Delete the whole collection under Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage › Holo. Your listings on the market and with friends are removed when you turn sharing off or delete them.

Children

Holo can be used by the whole family. The open market is for adults only, and children can't be contacted through the app.
